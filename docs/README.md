# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's project management guidance for running initiatives from idea to delivery and continuous improvement. The documents provide a simple, repeatable framework for defining goals, aligning stakeholders, planning work, executing iteratively, managing risks, releasing changes safely, and capturing lessons learned.

## Process Summary

OctoAcme starts projects with initiation, where the team validates the business problem, defines success metrics, identifies stakeholders, and creates a lightweight plan. This stage includes a project one-pager, milestone overview, risk list, and rough effort/resource expectations, and it ends with a go/no-go decision before full planning begins. Once approved, work is converted into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, and a milestone/release plan. The planning process also defines the Definition of Done and the testing approach so work is clear, measurable, and ready for execution.

During execution, teams use an iterative delivery model with visible tracking through a project board and daily or weekly coordination rhythms. Pull requests are kept small when possible, linked to the relevant issue, include acceptance criteria, and must pass tests, linting, and review before merge. Daily standups focus on progress, blockers, and dependencies, while weekly delivery syncs and sprint reviews surface risks and confirm alignment. The project roles and personas documentation clarifies responsibilities across Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA/testing, and stakeholders so ownership remains clear throughout delivery.

Risk management and communication are treated as ongoing responsibilities rather than one-time activities. Risks are tracked in a risk register with descriptions, impact, likelihood, owner, mitigation plan, and status; cross-team dependencies are escalated through PM and product leadership when needed. Communication is centered on a single source of truth, clear stakeholder updates, and defined escalation paths for issues, blockers, and incidents. Release and deployment guidance standardizes pre-release checks, smoke testing, deployment windows, verification, rollback planning, and post-release communication to reduce operational risk.

After milestones or releases, OctoAcme emphasizes retrospectives and continuous improvement. Teams capture what went well, what could improve, and the action items needed to address gaps, with each action assigned an owner and due date. These follow-up items are tracked in the backlog or issue tracker and reviewed in project syncs so improvements are measurable, actionable, and repeated over time. This lifecycle creates a consistent operating model that balances delivery speed with quality, transparency, and stakeholder trust.

## How to use this folder

Use these documents as a shared operating playbook for projects, programs, and delivery work. The overview explains the approach, while the more detailed guides provide practical checklists, templates, and role guidance that teams can adapt to their specific initiative.
